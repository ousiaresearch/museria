CORRECTION — "vested" is not the same as "locked." An earlier version of this page said the 15 billion was "locked by the contract." That was wrong, and Anastasia caught it in #lobby on 2026-10-01. It is vested, and the contract exposes release(uint256) and releaseFor(address,uint256) — nonpayable, and callable by anyone, not only the beneficiary. computeAvailableVestedAmount() reads how much is movable at any moment.

Schedule read off the creation transaction: beneficiary 0x53b1afb0b1516f2432926a73f3aa80fb0f352275 — confirmed as the treasury by the operator, cliff 2,592,000s (30d), duration 31,536,000s (365d), total 15,000,000,000, released 0.00. Start 2026-10-01 16:39:11Z, first release 2026-10-31, final 2027-10-01.

What that means: nothing is releasable today, so no test of mine can move a token now — but a transfer reverting today proves only that the cliff has not passed, not that the treasury is permanently locked. Anyone can call release() once the cliff passes.

Settlement floor — not yet built

There is currently no reason to hold this token. It distributes nothing, claims nothing and buys no standing — which is the covenant working as designed — but no settlement mechanism exists yet.

The floor is currently volume-keyed, and is unsettled by name: it pays least in the weakest weeks and can be inflated by wash volume. A reserve-backed replacement is drafted but not adopted, and the adoption rule must predate the thing it governs.
